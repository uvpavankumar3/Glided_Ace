# Luck, Location, or a Leak? — The Gilded Ace

**Recommendation: don't replace the bottom 10.** Fix the location, inspect one machine, test the comp change, and keep $87,300.

**Method.** House win mixes luck, exposure, location and the machine itself, so we separated them. Luck = house win − theo, scored against each game's volatility (σ = √(Σbet² × payout variance); the z-scores are well calibrated, SD 1.09). Location = what the same game earns in this zone vs. floor-wide. Machine quality = theo per available hour vs. the same game in the same zone.

**Q1: Weak machines.** 9 of the manager's 10 sit in Back Forty, where identical games earn about 34% less per hour. Two were already retired. M-1120's slot was refilled with the same title and ranked last again. None of the remaining eight is weak against peers. The one machine-level problem is **M-1158**: it hits on 38.6% of spins vs. 31.5% configured (z = 124) and is $36k below theo. That is a lead to inspect, not an accusation.

**Q2: Demand.** Coin-in eased 6% from February to June, alongside lower Las Vegas visitation (context only). Weekdays and leisure-event weekends drive the swings; conventions barely register. When the Back Forty walkway closed, that zone fell 30% and the rest of the floor didn't gain. The new bank B-51 took $5.4k/day without raising floor theo.

**Q3: Downtime.** 5,021 machine-hours down cost $90.7k of theo (0.39%). Ridgeline is 30% of machines but 56% of faults. Waits doubled when technician shifts went unfilled (Apr 14–May 25).

**Q4: Players.** Carded players wagered $163.8M, got $148.2M back and netted −$15.6M, about equal to theo. 16% finished ahead. The top 10% carry 56% of losses, and locals (8% of players) carry 57%.

**Q5: Comps.** Comps cost $191k (menu value $683k). The May 1 cut halved a comp's points price, but redemptions rose only 3% and theo stayed flat. Meanwhile the comp value of unredeemed points doubled, from $12M to $27M.

**Model.** A forecast frozen on Apr 30 (weekday means plus events announced by then) scored MAE $46k (3.6%) on May–Jun, against $218k for a constant mean. It shows waits, not headcount, drive lost play, so we roster to peak days instead of buying a technician.

**Q6: $100,000.** Inspect M-1158 ($1,200). Refresh Back Forty wayfinding ($7,500; break-even at a 2.8% lift). Run a randomized comp-rate pilot ($4,000). Keep $87,300. Base case: about $11k of theo in 30 days, $41k over 90.
